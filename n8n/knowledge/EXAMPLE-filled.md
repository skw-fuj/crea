## Turnaround

- **Photo** jobs are delivered the **next business day**, by midday.
- **Video** jobs take **two business days** to deliver.
- **Rush** (next-morning delivery) is available on request, subject to the day's schedule.

## What's included

Editing, colour correction, sky replacement on request, one round of revisions, and files
sized for both web and print. Extra revision rounds, raw files, and virtual staging are
add-ons — flag those for a human quote.

## Booking process

1. Client sends the **property address**, the **package** they want, and a **preferred date/time**.
2. Cfilms checks availability and sends a **quote**.
3. The booking is locked once the quote is accepted.
4. Access details (lockbox code, tenant/owner occupied, parking) are confirmed the day before.

The assistant never confirms a time itself — it says a date "looks open, Cfilms will
confirm" and passes the brief across.

## Scheduling notes

- Shoots run **Monday–Saturday, roughly 8am–4pm**. Best exterior light is mid-morning.
- A standard shoot on site takes **45–90 minutes** depending on the property.
- Reschedules are free with 24 hours' notice.

## Payment

Invoice on delivery, 7-day terms. Card or bank transfer.

## FAQ

**Do you stage or tidy the property?** Light styling only — the property should be
photo-ready before the shoot.

**What happens if it rains?** Exterior shots reschedule if there's heavy rain; interiors go
ahead as planned.

**Can the agent be in the video?** Yes — add about 10 minutes on the day for a piece to
camera.

**Do you do commercial / retail / off-market?** Ask a human — it's outside the standard
packages.

**How do I get the files?** A download link is sent on delivery; files stay available for a
few weeks.
