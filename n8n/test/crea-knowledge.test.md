## What's included / not included

Included: editing, colour, sky replacement on request, one revision round, web + print sizes.
Not included: extra revision rounds ($40 each), raw files, virtual staging (add-on $30/room).

## Booking process

1. Send the property address, package, and preferred date/time.
2. Cfilms confirms availability and sends a quote.
3. Booking is locked when the quote is accepted.
4. Access details confirmed the day before.

## Scheduling notes

- Shoots run Mon–Sat, 8am–4pm. Best exterior light: mid-morning.
- On site: 45–90 min depending on property size.
- Reschedule: free with 24h notice.

## Payment

Invoice on delivery, 7-day terms. Card or bank transfer.

## FAQ

**Do you stage or tidy?** Light styling only; the property should be photo-ready.
**Weather?** Exteriors reschedule if heavy rain; interiors proceed.
**Can the agent be in the video?** Yes, add ~10 min for a piece to camera.
